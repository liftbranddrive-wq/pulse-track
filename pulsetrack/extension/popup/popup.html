How does tracking work?

Whole computer. After you clock in, we detect keyboard/mouse use anywhere on this PC — other Chrome profiles, Firefox, Edge, Excel, Slack, etc. We do not read what you type or which apps you use.

Keep Chrome open. PulseTrack must stay installed in one Chrome profile and Chrome should be running (can be minimised). Clock in from that profile once per day.

Idle reminders: ~5 min no input on the PC → nudge. ~10 min → warning. ~15 min → ghost: active timer pauses until Resume focus.

YouTube / video: Watching without moving mouse or keyboard for 15+ minutes will pause active time (ghost). Scroll or tap a key occasionally if you are still working.

Break / Pause: Use official buttons when you step away — do not rely on ghost for lunch.

Active
Time that counts toward your required hours.
Ghost
Clocked in but no computer activity 15+ min — not counted as active until you resume.
Idle
Between reminders and ghost — timer may still run.
